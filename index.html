A world that keeps every scar

RefinedVanilla is a persistent no-hacks semi-anarchy Minecraft server running 26.2. The world does not reset, so highways, ruins, rivalries, and player history stay part of the map. Java and Bedrock share the same world.

There are no claims, homes, teleports, kits, or staff-built safe zones. Raiding, griefing, and PvP are allowed, while hacked clients and unfair modifications are banned. It keeps the pressure of a vanilla anarchy server while protecting fair vanilla play.

Ready to play? Join RefinedVanilla today, or connect with the community on Discord.
