The Art of Base Hunting: Finding the Hidden

Tactics • July 2026
By: RefinedVanilla Team

On RefinedVanilla, distance and secrecy are the only walls. But every wall has a crack. Base hunting is a game of patience, observation, and technical knowledge. With NoSeedMining active, you can't rely on terrain scanners. You have to hunt like a tracker.

1. The Highway Trail

Most players aren't perfectly clean. Look for 'chunk trails'—chunks that were loaded months ago vs. fresh ones. In the Nether, look for cobblestone markers or random fire that hasn't extinguished. Even a single misplaced block 100k blocks out is a beacon.

2. Bypassing NoSeedMining

Because the seed is hidden, you can't use tools like ChunkBase to find structures. This means bases near strongholds or mansions are more secure—but also more rewarding. Look for biological tells: unnatural animal spawns or villager trades that shouldn't be in the middle of nowhere.

3. Portal Phasing

Search for portals within a 1,000-block radius of major Nether highways. Most players don't travel more than 500 blocks off-highway before building their Overworld link. Check for 'ghost portals' or lighting glitches that suggest a portal was recently active.
