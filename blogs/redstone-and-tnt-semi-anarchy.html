Redstone and TNT: Technical Semi-Anarchy

Technical • July 2026
By: RefinedVanilla Team

RefinedVanilla runs on a custom Fabric stack, ensuring that your complex redstone machines work exactly as intended. In the era of 1.20.5 and 1.21, technical gameplay has never been more powerful.

TNT Duping: The Excavator's Best Friend

We explicitly allow TNT duping for technical projects. Whether you're building a world-eater or a perimeter for your raid farm, our server performance is optimized to handle high-frequency explosions without the 'ghost blocks' common on Paper/Spigot servers.

Cannoning and Griefing

While we are semi-anarchy, raiding is part of the game. Building a reliable TNT cannon requires knowledge of server tick rates. On RefinedVanilla, ticks are stable, allowing for precise 360-degree cannons and shield-piercing projectiles.
