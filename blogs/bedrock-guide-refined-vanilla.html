Bedrock Guide: Connecting and Playing

Guide • July 2026
By: RefinedVanilla Team

Play RefinedVanilla from your phone, console, or Windows Bedrock edition. We utilize a high-performance proxy to bring the full Java anarchy experience to Bedrock players.

How to Connect

  • Server Address: refinedvanilla.net
  • Port: 19132 (Default)

Proxy and Mechanics

We use Geyser and Floodgate to bridge the gap. Note that while 99% of Java mechanics work, Bedrock players may experience slight differences in combat reach and shield timing. Always use the Bedrock proxy routes to ensure the lowest latency.
