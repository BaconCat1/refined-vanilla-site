Join on Bedrock Edition

Windows, Android, and iOS can add Refined Vanilla directly. Use the address and port below. Bedrock and Java players share the same persistent world.

Minecraft Bedrock 26.2

If your device uses separate fields, enter bedrock.refinedvanilla.net as the address and 19132 as the port.

Windows

Use Minecraft for Windows, not Java Edition.

  1. Open Minecraft and choose Play.
  2. Open the Servers tab and choose Add Server.
  3. Name it Refined Vanilla.
  4. Enter bedrock.refinedvanilla.net and port 19132.
  5. Save the entry, select it, and join.

Android

  1. Open Minecraft and tap Play.
  2. Open Servers, scroll down, and tap Add Server.
  3. Enter bedrock.refinedvanilla.net as the address.
  4. Enter 19132 as the port, save, and join.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Minecraft and tap Play.
  2. Open Servers, scroll down, and tap Add Server.
  3. Enter bedrock.refinedvanilla.net as the address.
  4. Enter 19132 as the port, save, and join.

Xbox, PlayStation, and Switch

These consoles do not provide a normal Add Server field for arbitrary servers. A BedrockConnect-style custom DNS or a LAN relay app on another device may make the server appear as a local or redirectable connection, but those are third-party workarounds. They can stop working after console or Minecraft updates, and Refined Vanilla cannot guarantee or support a specific third-party service.

For a direct add-server flow, use Minecraft Bedrock on Windows, Android, iPhone, or iPad. If you try a console workaround, review the provider carefully and never share your Microsoft password.
